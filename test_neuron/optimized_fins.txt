OPTIMIZED FIN COUNTS - NEURON (ASAP7, VDD=0.70 V, TT, FO4 load on Z0/Z1)
==============================================================================
Source: exhaustive-transition HSPICE testbench (test_neuron/runone.sh, 4860 events,
schematic level, no layout parasitics). Every design below: 0 mismatches, 0 glitches.

Area model: pMOS <= 3 fins and nMOS <= 2 fins fit in one finger (free).
A 6-fin pMOS or 4-fin nMOS needs a 2nd finger ("extra gate"). "XF" = number of
extra fingers over the whole flattened NEURON (FRONT is placed twice, so a change
inside FRONT costs 2x). Fewer than 3p/2n never helped (it only added delay).

Reference: current schematic (Nov 15) = 13 extra fingers, 48.9 ps worst.

Where each cell is used:
  NAND2Z   FRONT_A/XI1, FRONT_B/XI1 (Z -> ZA/ZB, big fanout)
  NAND2    FRONT_A/XI0, FRONT_B/XI0 (net5, internal)
  OAI21    FRONT_A/XI2, FRONT_B/XI2 (XN)
  OAI221   NCNT/XI1 (R1)
  OAI32    OUT/XI1 (drives Z0, FO4)
  NOR      OUT/XI0 (drives Z1, FO4)
  NOR3     UCNT/XI3 (V3)
  NAND3    UCNT/XI1 (V1)
  MAJI5M   NCNT/XI2 (R2), UCNT/XI2 (V2)
  INV      NCNT/XI0, UCNT/XI0, OUT/XI2-4

NAND2Z = a NEW cell (copy of NAND2) used only for FRONT/XI1. FRONT/XI0 keeps the
plain NAND2 at 3p/2n. Without the new cell, upsizing NAND2 also upsizes XI0, which
costs 4 more fingers (2 nMOS x 2 FRONTs) for no gain.

CHANGES FROM THE CURRENT SCHEMATIC (for any XF level)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  NAND2 (FRONT XI0): nMOS MM0, MM1 4 -> 2 fins (extra finger was wasted)
  NOR   (OUT XI0, Z1): pMOS MM1, MM3 6 -> 3 fins (6 fins loaded R1, slowed Z0)
  NOR3  (UCNT XI3):  pMOS MM0, MM1, MM2 6 -> 3 fins
  then add the upsizes listed for the chosen XF level.

Notes
  - XF=9 is NOT a step toward XF=10: XF=10 drops OAI32 MM4 back to 3 and adds OAI221
    MM6/MM9 instead. Go from XF=8 straight to XF=10 when adding area.
  - OAI32 MM4 (pin A pMOS, at Z0) stays 3 in the XF=10 design: 6 fins adds drain cap on Z0 (tested worse).
  - OAI221 pMOS and OAI21 nMOS upsizing made the design slower; keep them 3p/2n.
  - At XF=10 the top paths are within 0.6 ps of each other (36.1-36.7 ps); every
    further upsize tried (11-15 fingers) was slower. Re-verify after layout/PEX.
  - Regenerate a design: python3 sizevar.py NEURON.cdl out.cdl <args> (see fins/round*.txt).
